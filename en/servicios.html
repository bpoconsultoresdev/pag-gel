Services

Testing and regulatory compliance across the whole gaming lifecycle

Eight service lines covering everything from game math to jurisdictional compliance, built for operators, platform suppliers and regulators.

RNG & RTP Platform / RGS Sports betting Esports Cybersecurity Regulatory Geolocation Responsible gambling
01 · Game math

RNG & RTP Testing

We validate that the random number generator (RNG) and the theoretical return to player (RTP) of every game behave exactly as declared to the regulator and the player. This is the technical foundation of any game logic design compliance assessment.

What's included

  • Statistical validation of the random number generator
  • Simulation and verification of declared vs. actual RTP
  • Review of paytables and game rules
  • Technical report ready for regulatory submission
02 · Infrastructure

Platform / RGS Compliance

We evaluate the Remote Gaming Server (RGS) and the architecture behind the operation: from transaction integrity to full traceability of every bet, ensuring the platform meets the technical standards each jurisdiction requires.

What's included

  • Architecture and data-integrity audit
  • Traceability testing for transactions and bets
  • Validation of content provider integrations
  • Change management after compliance sign-off
03 · Sports betting

Sports Betting

We assess sports betting systems, fixed odds, live markets and sports trading engines, verifying pricing integrity, risk management and compliance with the rules of each market.

What's included

  • Assessment of fixed-odds and live betting systems
  • Review of non-sports, event-based wagering
  • Testing of settlement engine and risk management
  • Verification of market rules per jurisdiction
04 · Competitive integrity

Esports

We assess esports betting and tournament platforms, focused on competitive integrity, prevention of result manipulation and full traceability between game title, tournament and sportsbook.

What's included

  • Compliance of esports betting platforms
  • Competitive integrity monitoring
  • Review of result data sources
  • Alert protocols for anomalous patterns
05 · Security

iGaming Cybersecurity

Penetration testing, vulnerability analysis and security architecture review for platforms, mobile apps and integration APIs, aligned with best practices and frameworks such as ISO/IEC 27001.

What's included

  • Web, mobile and API penetration testing
  • Vulnerability analysis and remediation plan
  • Review of data and payment handling
  • Support toward ISO/IEC 27001
06 · Regulatory

Regulatory Compliance & Jurisdictional Advisory

We support the technical-regulatory file before regulators across LatAm: interpreting regulation, building documentation, transferring approvals and managing the relationship with the regulator.

What's included

  • Jurisdictional advisory (Colombia, LatAm)
  • Preparation of the technical-regulatory file
  • Transfer of approvals between markets
  • Monitoring of current regulatory changes
07 · Verification

Geolocation and Verification

We validate geolocation, identity and age verification solutions that ensure the player is operating from an authorized jurisdiction — a core requirement of most remote gaming licenses.

What's included

  • Validation of geolocation providers
  • Identity (KYC) and age verification testing
  • Assessment of anti-spoofing mechanisms
  • Compliance report per jurisdiction
08 · Player protection

Responsible Gambling / Player Protection

We assess the platform's responsible gambling tools: deposit and time limits, self-exclusion, alert messaging and help flows, verifying they work exactly as communicated to the player.

What's included

  • Testing of deposit, loss and time limits
  • Validation of self-exclusion and blocking
  • Review of alert messages and player notices
  • Verification of help and referral channels
