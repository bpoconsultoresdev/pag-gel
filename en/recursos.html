FAQ

Answering your questions before you start

How long does a certification take?

It depends on scope and jurisdiction, but we set a clear timeline from the initial proposal, before any testing begins.

Do you work with operators who aren't licensed yet?

Yes. We support both operators already live who need to maintain compliance, and those entering a regulated market for the first time.

What's the difference between product and platform certification?

Product certification evaluates a specific game or betting market; platform certification evaluates the full system (RGS) that supports them. Most operations require both.

Can you certify across several markets at once?

Yes, we design the process for multi-jurisdiction expansion, reusing technical evidence whenever the regulatory framework allows it.

What do I need to request a proposal?

Just tell us about your product and target market through the contact form — we'll guide you through the rest of the process.
