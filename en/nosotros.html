Who we serve

Three audiences, one technical standard

Operators

Online casinos, sportsbooks and gaming platforms that need to bring their product into compliance to enter or stay in a regulated market.

  • Support before the local regulator
  • Ongoing compliance monitoring

Platform suppliers

Game studios, RGS providers and sports betting platforms looking to bring their technology into compliance across multiple markets.

  • Transfer of approvals between markets
  • Technical support for updates
